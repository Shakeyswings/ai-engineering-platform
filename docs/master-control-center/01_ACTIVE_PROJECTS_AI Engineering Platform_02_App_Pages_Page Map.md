# Page Map

Required MVP pages:
1. Dashboard
2. New Mission
3. Mission Workspace
4. Research Engine
5. App Builder
6. Evaluation Lab
7. Workflow Library
8. Approvals
9. Risk Register
10. Settings

Dashboard:
Shows active missions, recent evaluations, pending approvals, saved workflows, and risk alerts.

New Mission:
Captures goal, task type, priority, context, constraints, output needed, and success criteria.

Mission Workspace:
Main execution screen for plan, research, build, evaluate, patch, save, and logs.

Research Engine:
Turns questions into evidence-based findings.

App Builder:
Turns app ideas into product requirements, pages, database, backend, frontend, and deployment plans.

Evaluation Lab:
Scores AI outputs and records pass/review/fail results.

Workflow Library:
Stores reusable prompts, workflows, templates, outputs, and regression tests.

Approvals:
Handles human approval gates.

Risk Register:
Tracks risk items, severity, mitigation, and status.

Settings:
Stores workspace preferences and integration configuration.
